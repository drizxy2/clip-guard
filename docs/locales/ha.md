# Clip Guard

Clip Guard ƙarin manhaja ne na gwaji na burauza da ke yin gargaɗi kan umarnin shell da ake zargi kafin a rubuta su a allo ko a liƙa su a shafi. Ƙididdigarsa na iya yin gargaɗin ƙarya ko kasa gano barazana; ba ya maye gurbin kariyar tsarin aiki.

## Shigarwa

Sauke kuma buɗe [sabon sigar](https://github.com/drizxy2/clip-guard/releases).

- **Chrome, Edge, Brave da sauran burauzan Chromium:** buɗe `chrome://extensions` ko `edge://extensions`, kunna Developer mode, zaɓi **Load unpacked**, sannan ka zaɓi babban fayil ɗin da aka buɗe mai ɗauke da `manifest.json`.
- **Firefox:** har sai an sa hannu kuma an wallafa ƙarin manhajar a AMO, loda ta na ɗan lokaci a `about:debugging#/runtime/this-firefox` ta zaɓar `manifest.json`. Ana cire ƙarin manhajar wucin gadi idan an rufe Firefox.

## Kariya da sirri

Yanayin toshewa yana dakatar da rubutawa ko liƙawa da ake zargi har sai ka yanke shawara. Yanayin gargaɗi kawai yana yarda da aikin kuma yana nuna gargaɗi. Clip Guard ba ya aika buƙatun hanyar sadarwa kuma ba ya adana rubutun allo. Idan an kunna ma'aunin cikin gida na zaɓi, ana rubuta tushe, mataki, dalilai da kwanan wata kawai. Rubutun da ke jiran shawararka yana zama a ƙwaƙwalwa na ɗan lokaci.

Fuskar amfani tana bin yaren burauzarka. Duba [README na aikin](../../README.md) don cikakkun bayanan fasaha.
